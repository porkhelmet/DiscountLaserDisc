## On May 29th, 2026 Chuck visited with fans and staff of the Criterion Closet as it stopped in Portland during its national tour.  Criterion describes its closeted version this way: "Stocked with more than 1,000 of the greatest films from around the world, the Criterion Closet may offer more cinematic inspiration per square foot than any other place on the planet."  'Nuff said.
<br>
## It was a real thrill for Chuck to visit with fellow movie and laserdisc fans and Chuck couldn't help but bring a few samples and a cake to share as you can see.
